# ✦ DANCE THE NIGHT AWAY

### Dance Series · Track 10

A little midnight magic for your next event DP. **Dance the Night Away** is a lightweight, browser-based photo editor from the **Lasallian Pointes N' Flexes Dance Company (LPNFDC)**. Add a photo, find your frame, and download a ready-to-share profile picture.

> **Your photo stays on your device.** Framing and export happen in your browser; the project does not upload your image to a server.

---

## ✧ The experience

1. **Add your photo** — choose a JPG, PNG, or WebP, or drop it into the upload area.
2. **Find your framing** — drag to reposition, pinch to zoom and rotate on touch screens, or use the controls to adjust your photo within the event frame’s opening. Reset anytime.
3. **Take it with you** — download the finished event DP as a **1080 × 1080 PNG**, or open it in a new tab to press and hold the image to save it on mobile.

Square photos are a natural fit, but photos of any shape can be cropped. Uploads are limited to **15 MB**.

## ☾ Start the music

No install, build step, or dependencies needed.

1. Keep the `assets` folder beside `index.html`.
2. Open `index.html` in a modern browser.
3. Choose or drop in a photo and make your event DP.

To share the editor with others, publish the project files together on any static web host. The page uses relative paths for its styles, script, and artwork.

## ✦ In the spotlight

- **`index.html`** — page structure and accessible editor controls
- **`styles.css`** — responsive, starry-night styling
- **`app.js`** — photo loading, crop controls, preview, and PNG export/download
- **`assets/overlay.png`** — the event frame composited over the photo
- **`assets/`** — event artwork and LPNFDC logos

## ♡ A note on privacy

Photos are read locally by the browser and drawn to a canvas for the preview and download. No upload service or backend is included. Your browser may keep a local preview in memory while the page is open; removing the photo clears the editor.
